2026 Digital Experience Pipeline
RWE Competence Centre · Digital Experience
Scope: This pipeline covers the high-level, overarching initiatives only. Smaller tasks, day-to-day work and ad-hoc requests are handled outside this plan and are not listed here.

Priorities & Goals

Content gap closure: Identify and fill information gaps to ensure comprehensive topical authority.
UX excellence: Enhance user experience through intuitive content structures and layouts.
Performance optimization: Systematic SEO and CRO of all assets.
Journey-centric planning: Transition to a full-funnel content strategy aligned with the customer lifecycle.

Strategic Execution

The year is structured as a four-phase build, each quarter advancing the websites from foundation to full-funnel maturity across all RWE markets.

Q1 · Laying the Foundations — establish operational workflows, editorial alignment and keyword-driven optimisation.

Q2 · Joining & Assembling — build out category and application content, and grow team capability through SEO/SXO training.

Q3 · Finishing & Refining — scale content, prepare assets for the First Spirit migration and protect SEO continuity.

Q4 · Opening New Rooms — review performance, close out pending category builds and set KPIs for the year ahead.

Foundation & Alignment — Formalize operational workflows, align local efforts with HWW editorial standards, launch keyword-driven optimization.
